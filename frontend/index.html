How check-in works

  1. Your instructor opens a session and shows a code such as K7Q2XM on the screen.
  2. You type the code on the Check in page while the session is open.
  3. When the instructor closes the session, everyone who has not checked in is marked absent.

No account yet? Accounts are created by your department's administrator. There is no sign-up page.
